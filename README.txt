# StudyVibes — Website Setup Guide
=====================================

## Kya hai?
Gen Z aesthetic education website — Results, Past Papers, Admissions,
Scholarships guides + Study Blog. AdSense-ready structure.

## Files
- index.html ............ Home
- results.html .......... Results hub (3 guides)
- past-papers.html ...... Past papers hub (3 guides)
- admissions.html ....... Admissions hub (2 guides)
- scholarships.html ..... Scholarships hub (2 guides)
- blog.html ............. Blog listing (4 articles)
- <guide>.html .......... 14 guide/article pages
- about.html / contact.html / privacy-policy.html
- ads.txt ............... AdSense (publisher ID set)
- sitemap.xml / robots.txt (SEO)

## AdSense approval tips
- Privacy Policy + About + Contact pehle se hain.
- 14 original guides + 4 blog articles = strong content.
- Site live rakhein 2-3 hafte, phir AdSense ke liye apply karein.
- Apne ads par khud click NA karein.
- Dates/deadlines: guides mein koi fake date nahi likhi — real dates hamesha
  official websites se confirm karke update karein.
