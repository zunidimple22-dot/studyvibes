🎯 Matric Ke Baad College Admission: Field Kaise Choose Karein?

FSc Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, ICS, ICOM ya FA? Har field kya hai, kis ke liye hai, aur ghalat field se kaise bachein.

Advertisement

Matric ke baad sab se bara sawal: "Ab kis field mein jayein?" Ye faisla agle 2 saal aur phir university ki poori direction tay karta hai — is liye soch samajh kar karein.

Fields Kya Kya Hain?

  • FSc Pre-Medical: Biology + Chemistry + Physics. Medical (MBBS, BDS, Nursing, Pharmacy) ki taraf jane ke liye.
  • FSc Pre-Engineering: Maths + Chemistry + Physics. Engineering, CS aur technical fields ke liye.
  • ICS (Computer Science): Computer + Maths/Physics/Stats. CS, Software Engineering aur IT ki taraf.
  • ICOM (Commerce): Accounting + Business. BBA, Commerce aur banking ki taraf.
  • FA (Arts): Humanities subjects. Law, CSS, media, teaching aur arts ki taraf.

Field Choose Karne Ka Sahi Tareeqa

  1. Interest dekhein: 2 saal usi field ke subjects parhne hain — agar interest nahi to marks nahi aayenge.
  2. Marks dekhein: Science mein kam marks hain to zabardasti Pre-Medical lena aqalmandi nahi.
  3. Future options dekhein: Har field ke baad kaun se university programs khulte hain, wo samajh lein.

Ghalat Field Choose Ho Jaye To?

Ghabrayein nahi — 1st year mein field change ka option hota hai (college ke rules ke mutabiq). Lekin behtar hai pehle hi soch kar choose karein taake saal zaya na ho.

Field ka faisla doston ko dekh kar nahi, apne interest aur marks ko dekh kar karein. Doston ke fields badal jayenge, tumhara faisla tumhare saath rahega.
