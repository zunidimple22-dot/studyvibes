📚 Inter Past Papers (1st & 2nd Year): FA, FSc, ICS, ICOM Guide

FA, FSc Pre-Medical/Engineering, ICS aur ICOM — har stream ke past papers use karne ka alag tareeqa hai. Full guide.

Advertisement

Inter ke papers matric se zyada conceptual hote hain — is liye past papers ka tareeqa bhi thoda alag hai. Har stream ke liye alag strategy chahiye.

FSc Pre-Medical / Pre-Engineering

Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Maths mein numericals aur diagrams ki bohat ahmiyat hai. Past papers se numericals ke types samajh aate hain — aksar same style ke sawal repeat hote hain, values badal kar.

ICS (Computer Science)

Computer Science mein definitions + programming ke sawal aate hain. Past papers se pata chalta hai ke kaun se chapters se lambi questions aati hain aur kaun se se MCQs.

ICOM / FA

Commerce aur Arts mein theory zyada hoti hai — past papers se headings, format aur presentation ka andaza hota hai. Achhi presentation se marks mein saaf farq parta hai.

Pairing Scheme Kya Hai?

Boards pairing scheme (paper scheme) release karte hain jo batata hai ke kis chapter se kitne questions aayenge. Past papers + pairing scheme mila kar parhein to tayari focused ho jati hai. Pairing scheme apne board ki official website se confirm karein.

Entry Test Ke Liye Bhi Kaam Aate Hain?

Haan! Inter ke concepts hi MDCAT, ECAT aur doosre entry tests ki bunyad hain. Inter ke past papers achhi tarah hal karne se entry test ki tayari bhi mazboot hoti hai.
